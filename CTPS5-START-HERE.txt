CTPS5 0.1.0-rc3 — public source release

Based on Egg of Time by Power Pudu. Start with README.md and docs/BUILDING.md.
Build locally with your own USA YQUE NDS file. Then follow docs/INSTALLING.md.
No ROM, extracted assets, generated game C, saves or game executable is supplied.

RC2 corrects RC1 room-edge filling defaults and removes blurred side copies.
Console confirmation of this correction is pending. Keep your data directory
and existing saves; do not delete all settings to apply the one-time migration.

RC3 adds PS5 prompts in the game, including shared sprites and explicit
instructions. Fully relaunch after changing controller bindings. The in-game
art is generated in memory from your own verified NDS; no separate pack.
